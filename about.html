Engineering Philosophy & Learnings

The Kcrish Automation Suite was architected to bridge the gap between irregular supplier spreadsheets and structured accounting databases without relying on fragile server connections.

Core Architectural Learning: Bypassing browser-to-desktop network ports in favor of Client-side SheetJS Parsing + Standardized CSV Export completely eliminates CORS errors and environment-specific security roadblocks.

System Stack & Technologies:

Vanilla HTML5 & CSS3 SheetJS (XLSX) Engine Smart Auto-Mapper LocalStorage Sessions
